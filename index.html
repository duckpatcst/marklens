      How should MarkLens read your pages?
      Google Lens · how it works

      Take photos of every page, then let Google Lens read them

      1. Add the photosTap Take photo or Choose photos for the question paper and the exam sheet, as many pages as you need.
      2. Tap Read with Google LensMarkLens opens the first photo in Google Lens.
      3. In Lens, copy the textTap Text → Select all → Copy, then come back. MarkLens picks the text up and opens the next photo.
      4. See where marks wentAfter the last page, MarkLens matches the marks to the question paper and fills the dashboard.

      Lens is Google's app, so photos you open in it go to Google under Google's terms. MarkLens only receives the text you copy. MarkLens's AI then sorts that text into questions and marks. No photo is sent to it.

      OMR sheets are read on this phone: free, private and no AI. Print blank sheets with Print OMR sheets, scan one filled with the right answers (or type them), then scan each student's sheet.

      Upload at your own risk. Photos, text from Google Lens and answers that MarkLens's AI reads, sorts or grades go to an outside AI company (the AI provider). They may be saved and used to improve its output. When you ask for correct answers, the question text is also searched on the web. Crop out your name, roll number and school first. The free on-phone reader keeps photos on your device. Privacy notice
