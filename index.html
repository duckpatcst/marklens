How should MarkLens read your pages?
Google Lens · for every page

Let Lens read the page, then paste the text here

  1. Open LensIn the Google app tap the camera icon, or open Google Lens, or Google Translate → Camera.
  2. Point at one pageHold it flat with all four edges in view, then tap Text.
  3. Copy it allTap Select all, then Copy.
  4. Paste it hereCome back and tap Paste page under the paper or the sheet. Do the next page the same way.

Lens is Google's app, so the page you scan goes to Google under Google's terms. MarkLens only receives the text you paste. MarkLens's AI then sorts that text into questions and marks. No photo is sent.

Upload at your own risk. Photos, pasted text and answers that MarkLens's AI reads, sorts or grades go to an outside AI company (the AI provider). They may be saved and used to improve its output. Crop out your name, roll number and school first. The free on-phone reader keeps photos on your device. Privacy notice
