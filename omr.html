For the best reading
  • Print at 100% on A4 paper. Photocopies work too. Up to 200 questions fit on one sheet, enough for NEET's 180.
  • Students fill one bubble per question completely with a dark pen or pencil, and rub out changes fully.
  • For the answer key, fill one sheet with the correct answers and scan it in MarkLens first.
  • When photographing, keep the sheet flat and the four black squares inside the photo.
