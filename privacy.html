Privacy notice

What happens to your photos

MarkLens can read your question paper and exam sheet in three ways. One keeps everything on your phone. One uses Google Lens, then sends only the copied text to an outside AI company. One sends photos to an outside AI company.

Upload at your own risk. When you use the AI reader, AI grading, or paste text from Google Lens, your photos or text are sent to an outside AI company. MarkLens cannot control what that company does with them, and it may save them. Do not upload anything you would not want kept by someone else.

What the AI companies say they do

  • Groq (default). Groq's services agreement says it does not use what you send to train AI models. It may process and briefly keep requests to run the service, keep it reliable and check misuse. Read Groq's services agreement.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI (backup). When Groq is busy, the same AI model runs on Cloudflare, the company that already hosts MarkLens. Read Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Gemini (only if the site owner switches it on). On its free service, Google says it uses what you send to provide, improve and develop its products, and that human reviewers may read it. Google also says its API must not be used in apps likely to be used by people under 18. Read Google's Gemini API terms.

These companies can change their terms at any time. Their own pages are the final word.

What MarkLens itself keeps

  • Your question paper text, marks and dashboard are saved only in this browser, on this device, so they are still there next time. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.
  • This website's server passes AI requests through without saving the photos or text. To share the AI fairly and stop abuse, it gives each browser a random device id (a cookie that says nothing about you) and counts how many AI requests that device makes each day. The counts are deleted after a few weeks at most.
  • MarkLens has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.

Protect yourself before uploading

  • Cover or crop out your name, roll number, school, signature and face.
  • Photograph only the pages you need.
  • If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before using the AI reader.
  • If you are unsure, use the on-phone reader. It works without sending anything.

Grades are drafts

AI grading can be wrong. Treat it as practice feedback, not an official mark, and check it against your teacher's marking scheme.

Changing your mind

You can stop using the AI reader at any time by choosing On this phone · free. Photos already sent to an AI company cannot be recalled by MarkLens; contact that company about their data.
